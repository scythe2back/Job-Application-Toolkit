Why this layout is ATS-safe
  • One column, no tables, text boxes, columns, icons, photos or graphics.
  • Standard headings that parsers recognize (Education, Experience, Skills).
  • Real bullets and right-aligned dates via a tab stop, not a table.
  • Times New Roman 11 pt, 0.75 in margins, plain text for contact details.
  • Upload the .docx (or save it as PDF from Word) unless the employer says otherwise.
Philippine application tips
  • No photo needed. Images can break ATS parsing. If an employer asks for a 2x2 photo, attach it separately.
  • Birth date, civil status and citizenship are common on Philippine resumes but optional. Share only what you are comfortable with.
  • Applying to a government agency? Many require the Civil Service Commission's Personal Data Sheet (CS Form 212) in addition to a resume. Check the job posting for the current form.
  • Letter (8.5 x 11 in, "short bond") and A4 are both common. Use what the employer asks for.
